# Privacy policy — X Abuse Shield

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

X Abuse Shield is a browser extension that hides abusive posts on x.com. It works entirely on your device.

## What the extension reads

- **Posts on x.com pages you open** — their text, author, and timestamps — to decide whether to hide them.
- **Account and post data that x.com itself loads into the page** (for example account age and follower count), read from responses x.com has already fetched. The extension makes no requests to x.com of its own.

## What it stores, and where

All of this stays in your browser's extension storage on your device:

- your settings, word lists and allowlist;
- accounts you muted, and strikes against accounts whose posts were hidden;
- a review log of hidden posts (text, author, reasons) for the last 30 days, up to 500 posts;
- daily counts of hidden posts;
- the AI model, if you turn it on.

You can clear the review log at any time from the review page, and removing the extension deletes everything.

## What leaves your device

Nothing about you or what you read.

- The extension has no server, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends no post text, account data or browsing history anywhere.
- The only network request it makes is downloading the AI model, once, after you choose to turn the model on. In builds that ship the model inside the extension, there's no download at all. The download fetches fixed model files; it sends no information about you beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address, which the hosting service sees).
- "Mute on X" clicks x.com's own mute menu item, on the page, when you press it. Nothing else is automated on your X account.

## Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published with the extension's source and noted in its release notes.
