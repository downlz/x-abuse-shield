X Abuse Shield hides abusive posts on x.com before they appear on your screen, entirely on your device.

**Firefox (desktop and Android):** install or update from **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/x-abuse-shield/)**.
**Chrome and Brave:** download `x-abuse-shield-0.2.6-chrome.zip` below, unzip it, then **Load unpacked** in `chrome://extensions` (Developer mode on). Updating: replace the folder's files and click reload; settings stay.

## What's changed in 0.2.6

- **Leaner panels on phones.** A hidden post shows a centred row of icon buttons — 👁 Show, ✓ Not abusive, Mute here (person with a minus) and Mute on X (muted speaker with an X) — with the reason on the line below, instead of four text buttons wrapping over several lines. Desktop is unchanged.
- On phones, the AI model card now names the five languages the phone model covers (English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu, Bengali) instead of "34 languages".
- README: Firefox Add-ons link and badges.
