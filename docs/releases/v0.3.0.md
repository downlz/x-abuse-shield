X Abuse Shield hides abusive posts on x.com before they appear on your screen, entirely on your device.

**Firefox (desktop and Android):** install or update from **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/x-abuse-shield/)**.
**Chrome and Brave:** download `x-abuse-shield-0.3.0-chrome.zip` below, unzip it, then **Load unpacked** in `chrome://extensions` (Developer mode on). Updating: replace the folder's files and click reload; settings stay.

## What's changed in 0.3.0

- **Search results are filtered too.** Abusive posts are now hidden on x.com search results, on every tab (Top, Latest, Media…), and on hashtag pages. It's on by default; switch it off under **Settings → Where to filter → Search results**. Posts hidden there show up as "Search" on the **Hidden posts** page.
- **A lighter default AI model on desktop.** The new default, [DistilBERT](https://huggingface.co/gravitee-io/distilbert-multilingual-toxicity-classifier), is a **140 MB** download instead of 289 MB. It gives each post one toxicity score, in 15 languages including Hindi and Hinglish, and doesn't hide posts that only mention a group ("I'm a proud gay man…", Eid greetings).
- **Choose your model** under **Settings → AI model → Model**: *DistilBERT* (the default) or *Toxicity*, the previous model, which scores 7 kinds of abuse separately in 34 languages. Switching frees the old model from memory before loading the new one.
- **Phones are unchanged:** Firefox for Android keeps the light 93 MB phone model whichever model is selected, and doesn't ask you to download anything again.

## Updating

**Firefox:** Firefox updates add-ons automatically, usually within a day. To update now on desktop, open `about:addons`, click the ⚙ button and choose **Check for Updates**. Your settings, mutes and hidden-post history are kept. The update asks for no new permissions.

**After updating, on a computer:** the AI model switches to DistilBERT, so the popup shows **Download (140 MB)**. Click it once, ideally on Wi-Fi; until then the word lists keep working on their own. To keep the previous model instead, choose **Settings → AI model → Model → Toxicity**: it's already in your browser, so it loads without downloading again.

**On a phone:** nothing to do.

## Good to know

- DistilBERT gives one overall toxicity score rather than separate threat, insult or identity-attack scores. With it, only the **Toxicity** slider affects the model, and threats it finds are blurred rather than collapsed. Threats caught by the word lists ("I know where you live") are still collapsed. If you rely on the per-category sliders, choose the *Toxicity* model.
- DistilBERT is less sure of some Hindi (Devanagari) insults than of English or Hinglish ones. Try **Strict** if too many get through, and use **Not abusive** on the Hidden posts page to correct mistakes.
