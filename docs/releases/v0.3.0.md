X Abuse Shield hides abusive posts on x.com before they appear on your screen, entirely on your device.

**Firefox (desktop and Android):** install or update from **[Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/x-abuse-shield/)**.
**Chrome and Brave:** download `x-abuse-shield-0.3.0-chrome.zip` below, unzip it, then **Load unpacked** in `chrome://extensions` (Developer mode on). Updating: replace the folder's files and click reload; settings stay.

## What's changed in 0.3.0

- **Search results are filtered too.** Abusive posts are now hidden on x.com search results, on every tab (Top, Latest, Media…), and on hashtag pages. It's on by default; switch it off under **Settings → Where to filter → Search results**. Posts hidden there show up as "Search" on the **Hidden posts** page.
- **Steadier memory on long sessions.** The extension used to remember every post, account and text it had seen until you closed the x.com tab, including posts X had already removed from the page. It now keeps a fixed number and lets go of posts that have scrolled away, so memory stays flat however long you scroll. This matters most on phones.
- **The same AI model.** We tested three newer models as replacements. Each hid more harmless posts than the current one, especially in Hindi and Hinglish, so the current model stays.

## Updating

**Firefox:** Firefox updates add-ons automatically, usually within a day. To update now on desktop, open `about:addons`, click the ⚙ button and choose **Check for Updates**. Your settings, mutes and hidden-post history are kept, the AI model you downloaded is reused, and the update asks for no new permissions.

**Chrome and Brave:** replace the files in the extension's folder with the new version's, then click the reload icon on its card in `chrome://extensions`. Settings and the downloaded model stay.

Not affiliated with X Corp. The AI model is [Horizon-Labs/multilingual-toxicity-small](https://huggingface.co/Horizon-Labs/multilingual-toxicity-small) (Apache-2.0), converted to run in place; phones use a version with its vocabulary trimmed to English, Hindi, Hinglish, Urdu and Bengali.
